Tap the person button (bottom right) to load a .vrm model.

Info

  • Drag the avatar to turn it left/right or tilt it up/down, pinch to zoom, two fingers to move. Double-tap resets the view.
  • Bottom right: camera on/off, choose a .vrm model, and background colour.
  • Gear: tracking, expressions, performance and view settings.
  • Shirt: every texture on the model, with hide, replace and effects (animated GIF, APNG, WebP and MP4 work).
  • Eye: hides the interface. It comes back while you hover (or tap) the right edge or the bottom-right corner. There is no camera preview, so the camera feed is never shown.
  • .vsfavatar files are Unity bundles and can't run in a browser. Export VRM 1.0 from Unity (UniVRM) or Blender instead.
